# Challenge Brief

## Challenge

Create something that improves the perceived quality of rest.

The original prompt is: “Hack the Rest. 提升休息的质量感的东西” (“Hack the Rest. Something that enhances the sense/quality of rest”).

## Explicit Requirements

- The work should relate to rest.
- It should improve how high-quality or satisfying rest feels.

## Constraints

- No specific target user, rest context, platform, technology, data source, or implementation approach is specified.
- No public-data requirement or limitation is specified.
- No required deliverable format, judging criteria, or success metric is specified.

## Open Choices

Teams may determine the problem framing, audience, product or experience, implementation, and how to demonstrate improved rest quality.
